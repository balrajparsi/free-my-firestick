What you get

More space

Clears every app's cache, shows the biggest apps, and finds leftover installers from Downloader.

Only your apps

Lists what you installed, with sizes, and uninstalls only what you pick.

Fewer ads

Special Offers, the advertising ID, on-screen ad tracking, promo popups and autoplaying trailers: off.

Less spying

The services that upload usage, performance and logs to Amazon: off.

Faster

Turn off the Ambient screensaver (often the biggest RAM user) and halve menu animations.

Safe

Amazon apps are turned off, never uninstalled. Home screen, Settings, Appstore, updates and Alexa voice are never touched.

Install

On the Fire TV: Settings → My Fire TV → About → select the device name 7 times. Then Developer options → turn on ADB debugging (or Wireless debugging) and choose Allow when asked.

Open Claude Code (or another AI coding assistant) and say:

Clean up my Fire Stick with Free My Fire Stick (github.com/balrajparsi/free-my-firestick).

It finds your Fire TV, shows what takes space, and asks before changing anything.

  1. Download and unzip.
  2. Right-click start.command → Open (double-click after the first time).
  3. On the TV, tick Always allow and choose Allow.
